Profolio KSA design system

Every page, state and component here was cut out of the product's own render (profolio-reactjs, Bayut KSA tenant, fixture account) — nothing was redrawn. 485 components in 24 pages and 623 states, and the responsive layout (a phone, 375 wide) in 24 pages and 532 states; 1518 of 1723 component variants render identically to where they came from (≤1% pixel difference). One stylesheet per layout: profolio.css for the web, profolio.mobile.css for the responsive layout. Every screen and state in words, to find the one a PRD needs: the design knowledge base.

The redesign starts with My Listings. These are its tokens — the designer’s tokens.json (“Bayut KSA Profolio 2.0: My Listings mobile”, base 360pt, 2026-09-16) — held against what its build paints, measured off the 127 compiled states of the new My Listings (62 web at 1440, 65 phone at 360). Every other page keeps the current theme above. The values are in new-theme/tokens.css as --pf-ml-*; they replace no current token. The handover draws My Listings with its own header and rail; whether the product’s shell changes with it is [TBC].

Typefaces

Geist is the UI face (type.family.ui) and appears only on My Listings — nowhere else in the product. Figtree (type.family.chrome) draws the web page title, the empty states, the modals and most sheet and drawer titles; the Listing Performance drawer and sheet titles are Geist. Lato (type.family.app) draws only the phone’s page title, “My Listings”, and the artboard’s 9:41 status bar (the designer’s device frame). JetBrains Mono (type.family.mono) is painted nowhere. Arabic stays Droid Arabic Kufi; the handover has no Arabic.

Colour

The green is the primary now: the ramp runs −1 to 11 around the brand green (8, #28B16D). In the current theme the primary is teal (#006169) and this green is secondaryColor; the new My Listings drops colorPrimaryActive and builds its states from the green (pressed 10, ink 9, deep 11).
