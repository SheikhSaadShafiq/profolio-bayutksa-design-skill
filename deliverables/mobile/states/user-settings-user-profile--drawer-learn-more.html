Profile Picture Guidelines

A high-quality profile picture enhances your credibility and helps you stand out. The better the photo, the stronger your professional presence.
Your Profile Picture Should
  • Be a clear, high-resolution headshot with a solid background.
  • Be front-facing for a professional appearance.
  • Be centered around your head and shoulders.
Avoid Using
  • Selfies or casual photos
  • Group photos or full-length pictures
  • Low-quality, pixelated, or blurry images
  • Photos with distracting or cluttered backgrounds
  • Side-profile shots or pictures taken from extreme angles
