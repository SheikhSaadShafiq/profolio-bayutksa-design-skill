How to Earn Quality Score?
Listing Rank
Every listing is placed in a line-up of its similar listings: same property type, same purpose (sale / rent / daily rental), same area. The order is set by how often each one is seen by people searching on Bayut.
A listing
Seen the most, ranked 1st
Seen the least, ranked last
Lower is better
1st means seen the most
Once a day
The order is recalculated daily
After day one
A new listing gets its first rank once it has been live a day
Similar listings, Top & Average
Similar listings are the real competition: same type, purpose and area. Inside that group, two markers matter.
Average
Top listing
Fewer views, clicks and leads
More
Top listing: the single best performer in the group, the benchmark to beat.
Average: the typical listing in the group. Above it beats most of the competition.
Fair comparison, per day
A week-old listing is never compared against someone's year of history. Every comparison uses a daily average: total divided by days live.
