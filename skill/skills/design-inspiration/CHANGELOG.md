# Changelog — design-inspiration

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-01

### Added
- Mobbin first: screens for one screen, flows for a journey, sections for
  a website section. The platform is set per product (Mobbin's phone
  examples are iOS). Queries describe one screen or one journey in plain
  words. Every image is looked at before it is cited.
- The web when Mobbin is not connected, said plainly.
- The reference board (`references/board.md`): five or six cited examples
  from at least three products, what most do, and two or three directions
  built from the product's own components. Each direction carries its
  trade-off and marks its `[new]` parts.
- Mobbin's usage notices are shown word for word. A picture kept on a
  board is downloaded from its high-resolution link, and still cited by
  its Mobbin link.
