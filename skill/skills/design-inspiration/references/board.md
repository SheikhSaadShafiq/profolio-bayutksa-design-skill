## Picking examples

- Same job before same industry: an expense-approval queue can learn
  from a code-review queue.
- A product with the same audience (people at work, on a phone, in a
  second language) beats a famous one.
- Show the range: one simple, one rich, one unusual. A board where every
  example agrees is either a strong convention (say so) or a narrow search
  (search again).
- At least three different products; never more than two examples from
  one.
