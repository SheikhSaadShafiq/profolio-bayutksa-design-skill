# Changelog — design-animation-opportunities

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-01

### Added
- Where to look (`references/gates.md`): feedback gaps, state changes,
  spatial jumps, arrivals, attention, continuity.
- The four gates (frequency, purpose, speed, function). Every candidate
  passes all four or is rejected with the gate and the reason.
- The kept table, ranked, each with its recipe in the product's motion
  tokens and its reduced-motion version. The rejected list with reasons,
  and a verdict, which can be that no motion is needed.
- Hands the kept candidates to design-motion.
