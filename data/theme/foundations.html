My Listings — new theme Profolio 2.0 · not yet live · My Listings only

The redesign starts with My Listings. These are its tokens — the designer’s tokens.json (“Bayut KSA Profolio 2.0: My Listings mobile”, base 360pt, 2026-09-16) — following the build where the two disagreed (the designer’s decision, 29 Sep 2026): 12 tokens adjusted, 14 variants added, tokens.json’s own values kept in the table below. Measured off the 135 compiled states of the new My Listings (65 web at 1440, 70 phone at 360). Every other page keeps the current theme above, and so does the shell: the screens draw the product’s own header, and on the web its rail. The values are in new-theme/tokens.css as --pf-ml-*; they replace no current token.

Typefaces

Geist is the UI face (type.family.ui) and appears only on My Listings — nowhere else in the product. Figtree (type.family.chrome) draws the empty states, the modals and most sheet and drawer titles (the web page title is the product header's now — the current theme); the Listing Performance drawer and sheet titles are Geist. Lato (type.family.app) drew only the artboard’s own top — its status bar and title — which the product’s header now replaces. JetBrains Mono (type.family.mono) is painted nowhere (the only monospace text is the photo placeholder's caption, a stand-in for the listing photo). Arabic stays Droid Arabic Kufi; the handover has no Arabic.

Colour

The green is the primary now: the ramp runs −1 to 11 around the brand green (8, #28B16D). In the current theme the primary is teal (#006169) and this green is secondaryColor; the new My Listings drops colorPrimaryActive and builds its states from the green (pressed 10, ink 9, deep 11).

Decisions applied to the screens

The product shell is keptevery screen (135 of 135) draws the product's own header, and every web screen (65 of 65) its rail, in place of the handover's (scripts/theme/shell.mjs) — on a phone the rail is the header menu's drawer; the new theme is measured without them
The riyal is a glyph"SAR" written out became the official riyal sign (kit/riyal.svg, the product's icon-font glyph; the handover's own rough icon("sar") is redrawn the same way) in 152 places across 123 states — kept as typed inside the agent's own listing description (an editable field) in 6 states, by decision
The drawer is 780pxboth builds draw the Listing Performance drawer 780 wide (spec 02b agrees); 03 · F’s 680 is the Quality Score drawer’s width
Mark as Booked is derived5 screens composed in the build from its own booking values, tagged [derived], until the handover draws it
